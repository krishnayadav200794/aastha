About The Centre

Trusted diagnostic care with a human touch

Aastha Diagnostic Centre is a fully computerised pathology laboratory based in Kandivali East, Mumbai, offering pathology, microbiology, biochemistry, X-Ray and ECG services.

Alongside in-centre testing, we offer home sample collection for patients who prefer to be tested in the comfort of their own home.

Our approach centres on clear communication, a comfortable patient experience, and dependable turnaround on reports.
